### 2. Technical SEO

**What we implemented:**
- ✅ Proper page titles and meta descriptions
- ✅ H1/H2 heading structure (logical hierarchy)
- ✅ Clean URL architecture (`/orthodontics/braces` not `/page?id=123`)
- ✅ Canonical URL handling (prevents duplicate content issues)
- ✅ XML sitemap and robots.txt configuration
- ✅ Internal linking between related pages
- ✅ Image optimization (Next.js Image component)
- ✅ Mobile responsiveness (mobile-first design)
- ✅ Performance/Core Web Vitals optimization
- ✅ Structured data/schema markup where appropriate

**Why this matters:**
Search engines can easily crawl, understand, and index your website. Technical issues won't hold back your rankings.

## "So Will We Rank on Google?"

**Honest answer:**

The website is now **technically and structurally prepared** to compete for those searches.

However, SEO rankings aren't something anyone can honestly guarantee.

**Why?**

Google needs time to:
- Crawl your website
- Index your pages
- Evaluate your content
- Compare you to competitors

Rankings also depend on:
- **Competition** — Other dental clinics in the area
- **Authority** — Reviews, citations, backlinks
- **Google Business Profile** — Optimization and reviews
- **Ongoing content** — Fresh, helpful information
- **User signals** — How people interact with your site
- **Time** — SEO takes 3-6 months to show results

**What we've built:**
The **foundation** is now in place. Ongoing SEO is what builds on it.
